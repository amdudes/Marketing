Our Story

Delhi Art Community (DAC) was born out of a deep love for art and a desire to make it accessible, engaging and community-driven. Founded in Delhi in 2016, DAC has grown into one of India's most vibrant and trusted art communities.

We believe art is not just something you hang on a wall — it is a conversation, an experience, a bond. DAC brings together artists, collectors, connoisseurs and enthusiasts through weekly digital touchpoints and immersive seasonal events.

From silent art auctions on WhatsApp to exclusive member-only Season events with celebrated artists, DAC creates meaningful connections between people and the art they love.

DAC Seasons

Our signature Seasons are curated art experiences unlike any other — immersive evenings with celebrated artists, exclusively for our members.

Curated. Exclusive. Unforgettable.

Each DAC Season is a carefully curated event where members come together to experience art in an immersive setting. Past Seasons have featured master artists like Maestro Ajay De and many more celebrated names from the Indian and international art world.

Members enjoy live art demonstrations, private conversations with artists, curated art for sale, and an atmosphere designed to inspire and connect.

  •   Live artist demonstrations
  •   Exclusive artworks available for purchase
  •   Curated venue, food and ambience
  •   Private member-only access
